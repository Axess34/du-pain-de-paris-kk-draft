La maison · เกี่ยวกับเรา

Baked by the chef, every day.

Du Pain De Paris is a small, chef-owned boulangerie on Lang Mueang Road. Everything in our glass cases is made by hand in our own kitchen: flaky croissants, golden turnovers, Portuguese egg tarts, madeleines and financiers, all with butter from France.

Step inside and it feels like a corner bakery in Paris: warm earth tones, the smell of fresh pastry and just a few cosy seats. Stay for a coffee, or take a box home to share.
